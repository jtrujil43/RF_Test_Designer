CARDINAL GSG PROBE CONFIGURATION - 2026-10-30

Installed equipment supplied by the user: MPI TITAN GSG probes rated to
67GHz, with 150um between adjacent contacts. G-to-G span is therefore
300um. Each probe's three pads are collinear and have equal pitch.
Every landing pad is accessible from the substrate's top surface.

PROGRAM PORT MAPS

RDXTALK (four modeled ports)
  P1 WEST   aggressor near/input
  P2 NORTH  aggressor far/through
  P3 SOUTH  victim near
  P4 EAST   victim far

The central two lines stay parallel. Their fanouts turn the aggressor's
far end north and the victim's near end south. No signal crossover or
bridge layer is required. S21 is aggressor through, S31 is near-end
crosstalk, S41 is far-end crosstalk, all referenced to input P1.
Unused connected DUT/VNA ports must have their normal reference loads.

RDLLOSS, RFLEAK, TRLSET, ZCOUPON and RESONATOR2 (two ports)
  P1 WEST, P2 EAST; north/south probe heads parked/retracted.
  Multiple DOE/TRL rows are separate DUTs, measured one at a time.

STUBTEST (three ports)
  P1 WEST, P2 EAST and P3 NORTH at the shunt-branch end.
  Leave P3 open or externally short it only for the corresponding
  two-port reference response; calibrate all three planes for 3-port data.

VIAMATCH (one external modeled input)
  P1 WEST; east/north/south heads parked. RL/RX is the internal load.
  The program calculates input reflection, not a second external S21 port.

These numbers are logical DUT ports, not an assumption about fixed VNA
cable numbers. The east probe is logical P2 for a two-port coupon and P4
for RDXTALK. Use the corresponding channel/calibration mapping.
RDXTALK measurements need calibration/reference planes for all four probe
tips. TRLSET remains a two-port west/east standard generator; it does not
by itself generate a full four-head or adjacent-direction calibration set.

RDXTALK CONTACT COORDINATES

Lengths in this section are micrometres except coupled length L in mm.
The origin is the centre of the uniform coupled section. +X is east,
+Y is north. Let c=500*L, h=(DW+gap)/2 and a=actualFeed+PL/2.

  Probe   Signal pad centre X,Y       Ground offsets from that centre
  P1 W    -c-a, +h                    Y=-PP and +PP; same X
  P2 N    +c,   +h+a                  X=-PP and +PP; same Y
  P3 S    -c,   -h-a                  X=-PP and +PP; same Y
  P4 E    +c+a, -h                    Y=-PP and +PP; same X

W/E pad length PL lies along X, width PW along Y. N/S pad length lies
along Y, width along X. Both ground contacts bond to the common reference
plane. The program prints all twelve contact centres for the base gap.
For each .5x/1x/2x gap coupon it also prints h; the same a is retained.
Use that coupon's h in the formula above.

Default DW45, SG20, L5mm, PP150, PW80, PL100, minimum FL500 and PG1000:
  Actual feed length: 629.071829um; arm a: 679.071829um.
  P1 W signal: (-3179.071829, +32.5)um
  P2 N signal: (+2500, +711.571829)um
  P3 S signal: (-2500, -711.571829)um
  P4 E signal: (+3179.071829, -32.5)um
  Ground centres are 150um either side along the axes specified above.
  Adjacent N/E or W/S group-centre distances for .5x/1x/2x gap are
  1000, 1007.363607 and 1022.225176um respectively.

CLEARANCE RULES AND MODEL BOUNDARY

RDXTALK's minimum adjacent group-centre input PG starts at 1000um and is
adjustable in custom mode. That is a geometric starting value inherited
from the previous layout, not an MPI mechanical-clearance specification.
The exact TITAN body/connector style, positioner, skate and overtravel
envelopes are not supplied, so body fit is not certified by these scripts.

The actual feed is at least the requested FL, at least
2*PP-PW/2, and at least DW/2+(PP-PW). It is extended further if needed
to meet PG for the closest .5x-gap coupon. This prevents overlapping
orthogonal landing-pad rectangles and ground-pad/core intersections.
The implied pad-edge margin is PP-PW. Simple-turn layouts reject a feed
width that overlaps the GSG grounds or the closest victim trace; those
cases need a separately designed narrow neck/taper or different geometry.

Fanouts, the two turns, pads, transitions, probe-to-probe coupling and
their parasitics are excluded from the uniform coupled-line predictions.
Use matched controls and deembed the actual access structures. RFLEAK's
revised drawing connects the upper-left L1 trace to lower-right L2 by a
central via. Three east vias return G-S-G to the top-side probe pads;
ground rails have their own layer transitions. The retained isolated-plate
capacitance model does not model this via-connected drawing.

The 150um pitch and 67GHz installed-probe rating are shown in the reports.
Other custom pitches/frequencies remain calculation options and receive
an explicit installed-equipment mismatch notice. Existing analytical
model validity checks still apply within the nominal probe band.

SOURCE SCOPE

The eight physical RF layout programs in C64/ were updated. The matrix
and integer calculators have no probes. The parent-folder Hand BASIC RF
sources now share the cardinal layout/profile and retain mobile graphics.
RING67.TXT/PRG/README, supplied separately,
are preserved: their idealized two-port response omits physical feeds
and probes and does not calculate a probe layout.
